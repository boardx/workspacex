# Head of Growth & Lifecycle — PLG Experiments & Compounding Retention

## 🎯 Role Overview

Own full‑funnel growth from awareness to expansion with product‑led and community‑led strategies. Build an experimentation engine that compounds activation, retention, and revenue.

## 🚀 Core Responsibilities

### Strategy & Experiments

- Define growth model and loops (content → product → community → referrals)
- Run rapid A/B tests across onboarding, paywalls, pricing, and messaging
- Own lifecycle orchestration: emails, in‑product comms, nudges, and reactivation

### Analytics & Ops

- Build attribution, cohorting, and experimentation frameworks with Data/BizOps
- Manage martech and product analytics stack; define north‑star and guardrail metrics
- Partner with PM/Design on high‑leverage UX experiments

### Go‑to‑Market Alignment

- Coordinate with PMM, Comms, and Community for integrated campaigns
- Support Sales/CS with PQL, usage signals, and expansion playbooks

## 🎓 Required Qualifications

- 7+ years in growth for PLG SaaS; shipped material wins
- Deep analytics fluency; strong UX/product instincts
- Experience running multi‑variant experiments at scale

## 🌟 Performance Objectives

### Year 1

- Lift activation and D30 retention by step‑change improvements
- Ship 10–15 high‑impact experiments with documented learnings
- Establish lifecycle system with measurable expansion impact

### Years 2–3

- Mature growth loops; reduce CAC payback; increase NDR
- Build self‑serve + sales‑assist hybrid motions with clear thresholds

## 💰 Compensation & Benefits

- Salary: $200,000 – $300,000 + equity (0.05% – 0.25%)
- Performance bonuses for retention, NDR, and payback improvements
- Learning and wellness benefits per company policy

## 🌍 Cultural Fit & Values

- Data‑driven, user‑centric, fast‑learning, systems thinker
- Comfortable with ambiguity; values ethical growth practices

## 📈 Success Metrics

- Activation, D30/D90 retention, WAU/MAU, PQLs, NDR, CAC/LTV, payback

## 🧭 Role Metadata

- Level: Head / Senior Manager
- Department: Brand, Marketing & Communications (Growth)
- Reports To: VP Brand & Communications
- Location: Global / Remote
