# Hiring Scorecards — Brand, Marketing & Communications

**Purpose**: Standardize candidate evaluation across key roles to ensure skills, mission alignment, and cultural fit.

---

## 🎯 Evaluation Framework (All Roles)

### Core Dimensions (1-5 scale)

1. **Mission Alignment**: Personal connection to AI-native collaboration, consciousness tech, human potential
2. **Craft Excellence**: Role-specific skills (writing, strategy, analytics, etc.)
3. **Strategic Thinking**: Ability to connect tactics to outcomes; long-term vision
4. **Collaboration**: Cross-functional fluency; generosity; inclusive communication
5. **Execution**: Bias to action; ownership; delivers results under ambiguity
6. **Learning Velocity**: Intellectual curiosity; adapts to new contexts quickly

### Scoring

- **5 — Exceptional**: Top 5% of candidates; immediate high impact
- **4 — Strong**: Top 20%; solid hire with growth potential
- **3 — Adequate**: Meets bar but limited upside
- **2 — Weak**: Gaps in critical areas
- **1 — Poor**: Not a fit

**Hiring Bar**: Average ≥4.0 across dimensions; no dimension <3; strong mission alignment (≥4)

## 📋 VP of Brand & Communications

### Evaluation Stages

1. **Portfolio Review** (30 min): Narrative work, launch examples, media coverage
2. **Narrative Exercise** (60 min): Take-home: craft 1-page narrative for WorkspaceX mission
3. **Strategic Interview** (60 min): Category design, GTM strategy, measurement
4. **Executive Interview** (45 min): Leadership style, team building, crisis comms
5. **Values & Mission** (30 min): Consciousness tech philosophy, ethical AI, culture fit

### Scorecard

| Dimension                   | Weight | Signals                                                                                  | Score (1-5) |
| --------------------------- | ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| **Mission Alignment**       | 20%    | Personal connection to human potential, AI ethics, consciousness tech; authentic passion |             |
| **Narrative Craft**         | 20%    | Exceptional writing; clear positioning; memorable framing; category design thinking      |             |
| **Strategic Leadership**    | 20%    | Connects comms to business outcomes; builds measurement; thinks 3-5 years out            |             |
| **Media/Analyst Relations** | 15%    | Tier-1 relationships; coverage track record; analyst credibility; spokesperson prep      |             |
| **Team & Ops**              | 15%    | Builds high-performing teams; operating rhythms; vendor management; scales culture       |             |
| **Collaboration**           | 10%    | Partners across Product/Eng/Community/Sales; influence without authority; inclusive      |             |

**Key Questions**:

- Walk me through a category-defining narrative you created. What made it resonate?
- How do you measure comms impact? What's your north-star metric?
- Tell me about a crisis you managed. What did you learn?
- How would you explain WorkspaceX's mission to a skeptical journalist?

**Red Flags**:

- Shallow understanding of AI ethics or consciousness tech
- Over-reliance on PR tactics vs. strategic narrative
- Weak cross-functional collaboration examples
- No measurement mindset

## 🎯 Calibration & Hiring Committee

### Process

1. **Initial Screen** (Recruiter): Resume, portfolio, basic fit → 30-min call
2. **Take-Home Exercise**: Role-specific (see above); reviewed by hiring manager
3. **Panel Interviews**: 3-4 stages with cross-functional interviewers
4. **Debrief**: Hiring committee reviews scorecards; consensus or escalate
5. **Reference Checks**: 2-3 references (manager, peer, cross-functional partner)
6. **Offer**: VP approval; comp/equity aligned to level and market

### Hiring Committee

- VP of Brand & Communications (final decision)
- CPO or CEO (for VP role)
- Cross-functional partner (Product, Community, or Sales lead)
- Hiring manager (for IC roles)

### Calibration Guidelines

- **Hire**: ≥4.0 average, no dimension <3, strong mission alignment
- **Strong Maybe**: 3.5-3.9 average; discuss gaps and growth potential
- **No Hire**: <3.5 average or any dimension <3 or weak mission alignment

## 📊 Post-Hire: 90-Day Check-In

### Success Indicators

- **30 Days**: Onboarding complete; 90-day plan socialized; first quick win shipped
- **60 Days**: Owning a project end-to-end; cross-functional relationships established
- **90 Days**: Hitting baseline productivity; identified 1-2 process improvements; aligned on H1 roadmap

### Red Flags

- Not engaging cross-functionally; working in silo
- Missing deadlines without communication
- Cultural misalignment (not living values)
- Low initiative or ownership

### Intervention

- If yellow flags at 60d: Manager 1:1 to clarify expectations, unblock, and support
- If red flags persist: Performance plan or mutual decision to part ways
