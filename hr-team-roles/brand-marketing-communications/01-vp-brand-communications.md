# VP of Brand & Communications — Mission Storytelling & Category Design

## 🎯 Role Overview

Lead WorkspaceX’s global brand, mission storytelling, and communications to create and own the category of AI‑native future of work and consciousness‑integrated collaboration. You’ll transform our mission and vision into a coherent, resonant narrative across audiences (customers, developers, researchers, partners, media, analysts) and channels (brand, PR/AR, executive comms, content, social, events). This role shapes how the world understands human‑AI symbiosis, multi‑agent collaboration, and consciousness technology—and turns story into sustained adoption and growth.

## 🚀 Core Responsibilities

### Brand & Narrative Strategy

- Define master narrative, positioning, and messaging architecture that ladder from mission/vision to product value
- Establish category design POV for AI‑native collaboration and consciousness‑integrated work, including naming and frames
- Build and govern brand identity system (visual/voice) and usage guidelines across all surfaces
- Create the annual narrative plan and editorial calendar aligned to roadmap and research milestones

### Communications & Thought Leadership

- Own integrated comms: PR/AR, executive communications, social, blog, long‑form, speeches, and op‑eds
- Build tier‑1 media and analyst relationships; orchestrate briefings, launches, and narrative milestones
- Coach and prep executive spokespeople; develop keynotes, panels, and media training
- Stand up issues/crisis communications playbooks with Legal, Security, and Ethics

### Cross‑Functional Orchestration

- Partner with Product, Research, and Community to translate breakthroughs into accessible, credible narratives
- Align with PMM and Growth on launch messaging, content, and measurement; ensure consistency end‑to‑end
- Collaborate with Community/DevRel on ecosystem storytelling (agents, marketplace, open source)
- Integrate insights from Sales, CS, and BizOps to sharpen ICP, proof, and objection handling

### Excellence & Impact

- Build a high‑performing brand/comms team and agency network; set operating rhythms and KPIs
- Establish measurement for brand health, SOV, message pull‑through, and pipeline influence
- Ensure accessibility, ethics, and regional/cultural adaptation in all storytelling
- Manage budgets, vendor selection, and operational excellence

## 🎓 Required Qualifications

### Essential Experience

- 10+ years in brand/communications leadership at category‑defining or high‑growth technology companies
- Proven success crafting differentiated narratives and landing tier‑1 media/analyst coverage globally
- Demonstrated ownership of executive comms and high‑stakes moments (keynotes, crises, sensitive topics)
- Track record building teams, operating models, and measurement for brand and comms

### Preferred Background

- Experience in AI, developer platforms, productivity/collaboration, or research‑driven companies
- Familiarity with analyst ecosystems (Gartner, Forrester) and category design practices
- Global communications experience across EMEA/APAC and regulated/enterprise contexts
- Comfort engaging on ethics, privacy, security, and responsible AI topics

### Core Competencies

- World‑class writing and story craft; clarity under pressure; editorial judgement
- Strategic positioning; message architecture; executive presence
- Media/analyst relations; social/digital communications; content ops
- Program leadership; vendor/agency management; budgeting and metrics
