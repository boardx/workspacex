# Head of Product Marketing (PMM) — Positioning, Launch, Adoption

## 🎯 Role Overview

Own positioning, messaging, and GTM for WorkspaceX products and solutions. Translate research and product breakthroughs into value stories that drive activation, adoption, and revenue across segments and use cases.

## 🚀 Core Responsibilities

### Positioning & Messaging

- Craft solution narratives and value props per ICP, segment, and vertical
- Build messaging hierarchy and competitive story; maintain objection handling
- Partner with Research/Product to convert capabilities into customer outcomes

### Launch & Enablement

- Lead launch planning, readiness, and retros; coordinate PM/Design/Comms/Growth
- Produce assets: web, demos, decks, one‑pagers, use‑case blueprints, case studies
- Build Sales/CS enablement: battlecards, talk tracks, demo scripts, ROI tools

### Insight & Strategy

- ICP research, TAM/SAM/SOM insights, pricing/packaging experiments with BizOps
- Win/loss analysis; customer advisory councils; reference programs
- Content strategy for solutions pages, webinars, and playbooks

## 🎓 Required Qualifications

- 7+ years PMM in B2B SaaS; multi‑product/platform experience
- Demonstrated launch leadership and enablement excellence
- Strong research synthesis and crisp writing
- Comfortable with technical products and developer narratives

## 🌟 Performance Objectives

### Year 1

- Ship 3–4 launches with clear adoption impact and learnings
- Deliver complete enablement kit used by >80% of Sales/CS
- Establish pricing/packaging test cadence with measurable outcomes

### Years 2–3

- Mature solution playbooks per segment; scale case studies and proof library
- Strengthen competitive posture; improve win rates and deal velocity

## 💰 Compensation & Benefits

- Salary: $190,000 – $280,000 + equity (0.05% – 0.2%)
- Bonuses tied to launch quality, adoption, ARR impact, enablement usage
- Learning budgets for research/story craft; wellness and global collaboration benefits

## 🌍 Cultural Fit & Values

- Mission‑centric, user‑obsessed, evidence‑driven, collaborative
- Values clarity, integrity, and accessibility in all customer communications

## 📈 Success Metrics

- Adoption/activation, win rates, competitive displacement, ARR influence
- Content utilization, enablement NPS, qualified pipeline contribution

## 🧭 Role Metadata

- Level: Head / Senior Manager
- Department: Brand, Marketing & Communications (with Product dotted line)
- Reports To: VP Brand & Communications
- Location: Global / Remote
