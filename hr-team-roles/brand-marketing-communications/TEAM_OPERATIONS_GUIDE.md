# Brand, Marketing & Communications — Team Operations Guide

**Purpose**: Define how the Brand/Marketing/Comms team executes mission storytelling, launches, growth, and category creation in Q4 2025 and beyond.

---

## 🎯 Team Mission & Scope

Transform WorkspaceX's mission—AI-native collaboration, consciousness tech, multi-agent systems—into clear, resonant narratives that drive:

- **Adoption**: User activation, engagement, retention, expansion
- **Category Leadership**: Earned media, analyst recognition, thought leadership
- **Ecosystem Growth**: Developer/agent marketplace, community, partnerships
- **Revenue**: Pipeline influence, win rates, deal velocity

## 📈 Team KPI Dashboard

### Brand & Category (VP, PR, Content)

- **Share of Voice (SOV)**: % of category conversation; target +10pp per quarter
- **Tier-1 Coverage**: Monthly placements in target outlets (TechCrunch, The Verge, WSJ, etc.)
- **Analyst Recognition**: Gartner/Forrester inclusion; positive evaluations
- **Brand Health**: Aided/unaided awareness, consideration, preference (quarterly survey)
- **Message Pull-Through**: % of coverage using core messages; target 60%+

### Product Marketing (PMM)

- **Launch Quality**: Adoption within 30d of launch (activation, feature usage)
- **Win Rate**: Deal win % by segment/ICP; competitive displacement rate
- **Enablement Usage**: % of Sales/CS using assets; NPS on enablement quality
- **Content Impact**: Downloads, shares, time-on-page for solutions content
- **ARR Influence**: Pipeline/revenue attributed to PMM content and campaigns

### Growth & Lifecycle (Growth, Perf Mktg, SEO, Web)

- **Activation Rate**: % of signups hitting aha moment within D7
- **Retention**: D30, D90 retention; WAU/MAU ratio
- **Expansion/NDR**: Net dollar retention; PQL→paid conversion
- **CAC & Payback**: Customer acquisition cost; months to payback
- **Pipeline Generated**: MQL/SQL volume; ROMI (return on marketing investment)
- **Organic Growth**: Non-brand traffic, keyword rankings, backlinks, DA

### Ecosystem & Community (DevRel, Events)

- **Active Developers**: Monthly active devs; agents/plugins published
- **Marketplace GMV**: Gross merchandise value; creator revenue
- **Event Impact**: Attendance, NPS, pipeline influence, community growth
- **Content Utilization**: Tutorial/sample app usage; GitHub stars/forks

### Operations (Web/Martech)

- **Site Performance**: Core Web Vitals (LCP <2.5s, CLS <0.1, FID <100ms)
- **Conversion Rates**: CVR by funnel stage; A/B test velocity
- **Data Quality**: % clean records; SLA on data pipelines
- **Martech Efficiency**: Tool usage, redundancy elimination, ROI per tool

## 🔄 Cross-Functional Workflows

### Product Launch (8-Week Cycle)

**Weeks 1-2: Strategy & Planning**

- PMM: ICP research, positioning, messaging brief
- VP: Narrative alignment with mission/category story
- Growth: Funnel goals, success metrics, experiment plan

**Weeks 3-4: Asset Creation**

- Content: Blog post, whitepaper, case study drafts
- Copy: Taglines, web copy, social snippets
- PMM: Demo script, deck, one-pager, battlecard
- DevRel: Tutorials, sample code, API docs (if relevant)

**Weeks 5-6: Build & Test**

- Web: Landing page, conversion paths, analytics tags
- SEO: Meta, schema, internal links
- Growth: Onboarding flow, email sequences
- PR: Press materials, media list, embargo briefings

**Week 7: Launch**

- PR: Embargo lift, media outreach, analyst briefings
- PMM: Sales/CS enablement, webinar, launch event
- Perf Mktg: Paid campaigns live
- DevRel: Developer spotlight, hackathon kickoff
- Content: Publish blog, promote on social

**Week 8: Retro & Optimization**

- All: Metrics review, learnings, iteration plan
- VP: Stakeholder debrief, next milestone alignment

---

### Quarterly Planning Cadence

**Month 1 (Week 1-2): Strategy**

- VP: OKRs, narrative themes, budget allocation
- All leads: Team roadmaps, dependencies, resource asks

**Month 1 (Week 3-4): Execution Planning**

- RACI per initiative; DRI assignments
- Content calendar; campaign calendar; event calendar

**Months 2-3: Execute & Measure**

- Weekly team sync (metrics, blockers, wins)
- Biweekly cross-functional (Product, Eng, Community, Sales)
- Monthly business review (exec readout)

**End of Quarter: Retro & Plan Next**

- What worked, what didn't; pivot decisions
- Carry-forward vs. new bets

## 🚀 90-Day Onboarding Plans

### All Roles (Days 1-30)

- **Week 1**: Mission/vision deep dive; product walkthrough; meet cross-functional partners
- **Week 2**: Audit current state (narrative, content, campaigns, metrics); identify gaps
- **Week 3**: Shadow key workflows; review past launches/campaigns; customer interviews
- **Week 4**: Present findings and 90-day plan to VP and team

### Leadership Roles (Days 31-90)

- **Month 2**: Ship 1-2 quick wins; establish operating rhythm; build team relationships
- **Month 3**: Launch major initiative (narrative refresh, campaign, or process upgrade); set H1 roadmap

### IC Roles (Days 31-90)

- **Month 2**: Own a project end-to-end (blog series, experiment, or launch asset)
- **Month 3**: Hit baseline productivity; identify one process/tool improvement

- Top 3 brand in category (awareness, consideration)
- 50+ active community contributors; 1,000+ agents in marketplace
- Sustained organic growth (30%+ non-brand traffic)

## 🛠️ Operating Principles

1. **Mission First**: Every campaign, every message ties back to human potential and AI-native collaboration
2. **Data-Driven Storytelling**: Back claims with evidence; test and iterate narratives
3. **Community Co-Creation**: Engage developers, users, and researchers in storytelling
4. **Ethical & Inclusive**: Accessibility, transparency, cultural sensitivity in all comms
5. **Speed with Quality**: Ship fast, learn fast, but never compromise brand integrity
6. **Cross-Functional by Default**: No silos; align with Product, Eng, Community, Sales/CS
7. **Long-Term Thinking**: Build category, not just campaigns; invest in evergreen assets
