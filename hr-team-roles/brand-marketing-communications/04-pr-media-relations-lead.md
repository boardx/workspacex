# PR & Media Relations Lead — Earned Narrative & Executive Comms

## 🎯 Role Overview

Own earned media and analyst relations to shape category narrative and credibility. Prepare executives for high‑stakes moments and manage proactive and reactive communications.

## 🚀 Core Responsibilities

### Media & Analyst Strategy

- Build and execute media plan; pitch tier‑1 stories and narratives
- Orchestrate analyst briefings and submissions; track evaluations
- Manage newsroom operations: press releases, media kits, FAQs

### Executive Communications

- Develop talking points, op‑eds, speeches, and keynote narratives
- Media train and prep executives; run briefing centers
- Coordinate with Legal/Security on issues and crisis response

### Measurement & Operations

- Establish SOV, sentiment, and message pull‑through measurement
- Manage agency partners; optimize budgets and output quality

## 🎓 Required Qualifications

- 6+ years PR/AR in technology; track record with top‑tier outlets
- Strong writing; excellent judgement and calm under pressure
- Experience with global programs and sensitive topics

## 🌟 Performance Objectives

- Secure recurring tier‑1 coverage and analyst recognition
- Improve message pull‑through and executive comms consistency
- Build playbooks for rapid response and narrative defense

## 💰 Compensation & Benefits

- Salary: $150,000 – $230,000 + equity (0.02% – 0.1%)
- Bonuses for coverage quality, SOV, and narrative impact

## 🌍 Cultural Fit & Values

- Mission‑aligned, ethical, transparent, inclusive communicator

## 📈 Success Metrics

- Tier‑1 placements, SOV, sentiment, analyst outcomes, exec readiness

## 🧭 Role Metadata

- Level: Lead / Senior IC
- Department: Brand, Marketing & Communications
- Reports To: VP Brand & Communications
- Location: Global / Remote
