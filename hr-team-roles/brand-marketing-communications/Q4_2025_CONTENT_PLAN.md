## 🎨 Content Production Workflow

### Roles & Responsibilities

- Editorial strategy and calendar management
- Content briefs and writer assignments
- Editing and quality control
- SEO optimization
- Distribution coordination

**VP Brand & Communications**

- Thought leadership authorship (ghostwritten by Content Lead)
- Executive review and approval (major pieces)
- Strategic direction and narrative alignment

**Copywriter / Brand Strategist**

- High-stakes writing (CEO blog posts, whitepapers, manifestos)
- Brand voice consistency
- Headlines, taglines, social copy

**DevRel Marketing Lead**

- Developer tutorials and technical content
- Code samples and GitHub repos
- Developer community engagement

**Product Marketing (PMM)**

- Launch content (announcement blogs, messaging)
- Product-focused content
- Sales enablement assets

**Design Team**

- Visual content (infographics, diagrams)
- Video production (in-house or coordinate with agency)
- Layout and formatting (whitepapers, case studies)

**Subject Matter Experts** (CSO, CPO, CTO, Research)

- Technical accuracy review
- Research insights and data
- Interview subjects

---

### Production Process (Standard Blog Post)

**Week 1: Planning & Brief**

- [ ] Content Lead creates brief (audience, objective, outline, SEO keywords)
- [ ] Assign writer (Content Lead, Copywriter, or external)
- [ ] Research and interviews (if needed)

**Week 2: Drafting**

- [ ] Writer delivers first draft (80% complete)
- [ ] Content Lead reviews and provides feedback
- [ ] Revision cycle (1-2 rounds)

**Week 3: Finalization**

- [ ] Final draft to stakeholders for review (if major piece)
- [ ] Incorporate feedback
- [ ] Copyedit and proofread
- [ ] SEO optimization (meta description, keywords, internal links)

**Week 4: Production & Publish**

- [ ] Design creates visual assets (header image, pullquotes)
- [ ] Format in CMS (WordPress, Medium, or custom)
- [ ] Schedule publish date and time
- [ ] Prepare distribution (email, social, Slack)

**Day of Publish**

- [ ] Publish blog post
- [ ] Send email to mailing list (if appropriate)
- [ ] Share on social media (LinkedIn, Twitter, with custom messaging)
- [ ] Post in community channels (Discord, Slack, forums)
- [ ] Monitor engagement and respond to comments

**Week After Publish**

- [ ] Track metrics (views, time-on-page, shares, backlinks, conversions)
- [ ] Report results to team
- [ ] Iterate based on learnings

---

### Production Process (Whitepaper)

**Week 1-2: Planning & Research**

- [ ] Define objective, audience, key messages
- [ ] Create detailed outline (chapter structure)
- [ ] Conduct research (industry data, customer interviews, analyst input)
- [ ] Assign sections to writers (collaborative approach)

**Week 3-4: Drafting**

- [ ] Writers deliver chapter drafts
- [ ] Content Lead consolidates and edits for flow and consistency
- [ ] Technical review by SMEs (CSO, CPO, Research)

**Week 5: Revision**

- [ ] Incorporate feedback from SMEs
- [ ] Executive review (CEO, VP Brand)
- [ ] Copyedit and proofread
- [ ] Legal/compliance review (if needed)

**Week 6: Design & Production**

- [ ] Design team creates layout (InDesign or Figma)
- [ ] Create data visualizations (charts, graphs)
- [ ] Source or create illustrations and photos
- [ ] Finalize PDF and web version

**Week 7: Landing Page & Distribution**

- [ ] Build landing page with form (if gated)
- [ ] Set up email nurture sequence
- [ ] Prepare PR distribution (media list, pitch angles)
- [ ] Create social media assets (graphics, quotes, teasers)

**Week 8: Launch**

- [ ] Publish landing page and whitepaper
- [ ] Send email announcement to mailing list
- [ ] PR outreach to media and analysts
- [ ] Social amplification campaign
- [ ] Sales/CS enablement (briefing, how to use)

**Ongoing**

- [ ] Track downloads, leads, attribution
- [ ] Iterate landing page for conversion optimization
- [ ] Create derivative content (blog posts, infographics, social snippets)
