Retains short internal meeting scope (≤60 minutes), metadata, decisions, action items and uncertainty labels. WorkspaceX adds mandatory source locations, removes automatic external publication/task creation, and uses existing governed artifact output. Missing metadata is not an excuse to invent facts or unnecessarily block a draft. Owner/deadline fields may be Unknown; no unsupported inference from speaker voice. No standalone meeting engine or ASR client is bundled.
