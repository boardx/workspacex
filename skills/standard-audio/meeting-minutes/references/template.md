# Minutes template

Title / date / duration / organizer / attendees: factual values or Unknown.
Source: attachment identity, original hash or supplied notes; state missing evidence.

## Summary
Meeting objective and supported outcome in one short paragraph.

## Decisions
Decision; approving person/group if explicit; rationale; original quote and segment/range. Clearly distinguish proposed and confirmed decisions.

## Action items
Action; owner or Unknown; due date or Unknown; acceptance criterion if explicit; evidence location. Do not silently turn a discussion topic into an assigned task.

## Agenda notes and unresolved questions
Brief factual notes, blockers and questions. Suggested next steps must be labelled suggestions.

## References and revision
Actual sources only, draft revision and substantive corrections. For ≤30-minute meetings aim for about one page; for ≤60-minute meetings about two pages when content permits. Layout preferences never justify dropping important decisions.
