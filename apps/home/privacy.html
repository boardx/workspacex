Privacy · Security · Accessibility

What this website does, in full.

Short, because there is not much to say. This is a statement about the website you are reading, not about the WorkspaceX product — if you are evaluating the product, ask us and we will answer in writing.

What this site collects

Nothing. There is no analytics, no tag manager, no pixel, no A/B testing, no session recording and no advertising code. We do not know you were here.

Cookies

None. The site sets one key in your browser’s local storage, wsx.langhint.dismissed, and only if you dismiss the banner offering the other language. It exists so the banner does not come back. It never leaves your browser, and clearing site data removes it.

Third parties

There are none on this site. The fonts are served from this domain rather than a font CDN, specifically so that loading a page does not disclose your address to a third party. Nothing here calls out to another origin — a Content-Security-Policy on every response enforces that, rather than leaving it to good intentions.

Logs

The host that serves these files keeps ordinary web server logs, which include IP addresses, for a limited period and for operational purposes. We do not build profiles from them.

If you contact us

Email reaches a mailbox we read. We keep the correspondence as long as the conversation is live and for our records afterwards, and we do not add you to a mailing list you did not ask for.

Reporting a security problem

Write to security@boardx.us. Tell us what you found and how to reproduce it. We will confirm receipt, keep you informed, and we will not pursue anyone who reports in good faith and does not access other people's data.

Accessibility

This site targets WCAG 2.1 AA. Every build is checked automatically with axe-core in both languages and must report zero violations, and keyboard operation of every control is tested the same way. Two things are known and deliberate: the decorative diagrams are exposed as images with text descriptions rather than as data, and the scroll-driven sequence has a non-animated equivalent under prefers-reduced-motion. If something here does not work for you, write to hello@boardx.us and tell us what broke — that is more useful to us than a conformance badge.

Changes

This page is in the same public repository as the site itself, so its history is the change log.
