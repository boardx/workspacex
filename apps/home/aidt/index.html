01 — A direction for transformation

AI transformation starts with people.

Adopting tools is the beginning. Transformation changes employee workflows, customer experiences and organizational decisions.

Which problems deserve AI? Where does human judgment matter? How do new ways of working earn trust? Design thinking brings these questions into research, co-creation and experiments.

Find meaningful problems

Start with real customer and employee situations to identify where AI can create value.

Design human–AI collaboration

Understand people’s abilities, needs and concerns. Define AI’s role, human decisions and points of intervention.

Turn change into experiments

Use small prototypes and pilots to test practical outcomes, usability and business value before expanding.

Invite teams into the change

Bring different roles into defining problems and exploring solutions to build shared understanding through practice.

02 — The design thinking mindset

Expand your thinking. Keep your judgment.

Design thinking is a set of mindsets for complex problems. AI helps teams practice them, broaden their thinking and move into action.

Human-centered empathy

Organize interviews and feedback, identify needs across groups and develop follow-up questions. Verify findings with real people.

Curiosity and openness

Ask for alternative explanations, counterexamples and references from other fields to challenge familiar assumptions.

Reframing the problem

Examine challenges through user, employee, business and technology perspectives. Compare problem statements before choosing solutions.

Comfort with uncertainty

Separate what is known, unknown and assumed, then identify the next learning action even with incomplete information.

Experimentation and action

Generate storyboards, copy and prototypes to reduce the cost of trying and put ideas in front of people sooner.

Collaboration and co-creation

Bring together perspectives, make disagreements visible and help teams build a shared understanding.

Reflection and iteration

Compare expectations with test feedback and turn what the team learns into the next improvement.

AI can simulate perspectives. Real empathy comes from contact with people. AI-generated insights and suggestions need evidence and human judgment.

Explore each stage: tasks, outputs and human judgment
  1. 01

    Empathize

    Find clues in user voices. Use AI to organize interviews, synthesize feedback and map journeys.

    What you get

    Insight summaries, journey sketches and questions for further research.

    The human role

    Meet real users, understand context and check AI’s summaries.

  2. 02

    Define

    Turn complexity into a clear challenge. Compare explanations and distinguish symptoms from potential causes.

    What you get

    Problem statements, key assumptions and “How might we…” questions.

    The human role

    Set priorities and choose which problem is worth solving.

  3. 03

    Ideate

    Explore solutions from different roles, constraints and situations. Combine and compare ideas with AI.

    What you get

    Creative directions, solution comparisons and value hypotheses.

    The human role

    Choose based on user value, business goals and feasibility.

  4. 04

    Prototype

    Make ideas tangible with storyboards, page copy, interaction sketches and working prototypes.

    What you get

    A concrete version of the idea that can be shown and tested.

    The human role

    Define what the prototype should test and preserve the core experience.

  5. 05

    Test

    Use AI to draft test tasks, organize observations and summarize issues into clear iteration priorities.

    What you get

    Test findings, improvement priorities and the next validation plan.

    The human role

    Observe real behavior and evaluate the solution using user evidence.

04 — Co-create the change

Make AI transformation a shared practice.

Bring business, product, design, technology and frontline teams around one challenge. AI reduces organizing work and expands ideas, leaving more time for conversation and decisions.

See how AI helps before, during and after the workshop

Before: build a shared starting point

Use AI to organize research, interviews and business materials, surface preliminary clues and open questions, and help prepare the agenda and activities. Bring traceable evidence into the room.

During: deepen exploration and dialogue

Cluster notes, summarize discussion, propose alternatives and generate storyboard or prototype sketches. Anonymous input and perspective summaries can help more voices enter the conversation. Participants choose and decide together.

After: move from ideas to experiments

Organize concepts, decision rationale and unresolved questions. Help create experiment plans, responsibilities and follow-up materials so teams know what to validate next and how to measure it.

Workshop outcomes belong to the participants. Check AI’s summaries with the team, retain minority views and unresolved disagreements, and protect personal information in research materials.

  • A pilot and learning plan Define owners, validation methods and success criteria.

AIDT for higher education

Learn through real projects. Explore the future of higher education in China with AIDT.

AI is changing how knowledge is accessed and redefining the capabilities graduates need. Higher education in China faces a new challenge: helping students understand real needs, ask valuable questions, cross disciplinary boundaries and turn knowledge into actions and outcomes that can be tested.

Artificial Intelligence Design Thinking (AIDT) brings artificial intelligence and design thinking together: human needs set the direction, real projects organize learning, AI expands exploration, and sustained practice builds judgment and creativity.

Strategic partnership

Partnering with the School of Design Thinking, Communication University of China to advance project-driven education.

WorkspaceX and the School of Design Thinking, Communication University of China are strategic partners. Together, we bring design thinking in education, the exploration of CUC AIDT Lab — AI⁺ Future Creativity Lab, and human–AI collaboration into a shared initiative. Our collaboration focuses on courses, faculty development, workshops and real projects to develop an AIDT teaching model that can be practiced, evaluated and continually improved.

Explore the full learning journey

  1. Meet real people.

    Use interviews, observation and field research to understand needs. AI helps prepare questions, organize material and identify leads worth following.

  2. Define the problem together.

    Combine user insights with disciplinary knowledge to identify a worthwhile challenge. AI helps compare explanations and reveal assumptions and gaps.

  3. Expand the possibilities.

    Develop alternative ideas through co-creation. AI contributes cross-disciplinary references and perspectives that help teams move beyond their first ideas.

  4. Make ideas tangible.

    Turn ideas into storyboards, service journeys, interactive prototypes or working demonstrations. AI shortens the path from an idea to something people can experience.

  5. Test in real situations.

    Invite people to try the work, gather feedback and improve it. Use evidence to judge progress and reflect on what to learn next.

Each stage leaves a traceable learning record: how the problem was understood, why a solution was chosen and how the team changed its judgment all become part of teaching.

Explore teaching mindsets, cooperation and assessment

Build the mindset to face uncertainty in the AI era.

Experimentation encourages early testing, while reflection helps students recognize bias and take responsibility.

AI can help students form more hypotheses, compare more possibilities and receive timely feedback. Students must verify information, explain their choices and take responsibility for their work. Real interviews, field observation and user feedback remain essential evidence for learning.
